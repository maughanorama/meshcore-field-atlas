FIELD NOTES  /  DATA & METHOD

Reading the atlas

Nodes. All advertised node types from the public MeshCore map whose published coordinates fall within the ONS December 2024 UK country boundaries. This includes Northern Ireland and UK islands; it excludes the Crown Dependencies, offshore points and nodes without usable locations. Listings are not a live activity check. Nearby or co-located nodes remain separate directory entries.

Postcodes. Great Britain: OS Code-Point Open, with online Postcodes.io fallback. Northern Ireland: Postcodes.io / ONSPD (online). A postcode is a representative point, not a surveyed antenna position.

Terrain. Great Britain: OS Terrain 50 (May 2026), stored to whole metres. Northern Ireland: Copernicus DEM GLO-90, a surface model that can include vegetation and buildings (EGM2008). For sea crossings and gaps, regional AWS Terrain Tiles at zoom 8 are used, with submerged terrain set to sea level. Dataset transitions may have small vertical differences.

Model. WGS84 geodesic, sampled every 50 m or less and interpolated from the available grid. The dashed line uses a 4/3 effective Earth radius and the selected heights. A terrain-only pass is not a coverage prediction: obstacles, Fresnel clearance, antenna properties and radio settings matter. Very long paths are illustrative, not practical single-hop predictions.
